# Verify a generated video before you deliver it

## Compare faces against the locked references

- Open each locked reference image beside the finished video and compare every named character face by face.

- Check head shape and face proportions and eyes and mouth and skin or fur texture and outfit and distinguishing marks for each character.

- Reject the video when any named face drifts from its reference even when the action and framing look good.

- Fix one face first and show that fix before you rebuild the rest when several faces are wrong.

## Listen to the full audio at natural pace

- Play the full audio from start to end at 1.0x speed with no pitch shift and no speed up.

- Check that every dialogue line is present and intelligible and that the accent matches the requested accent.

- Check that only the labeled speaker moves the mouth for each line and that music and effects do not bury the voice.

- Shorten the script when the natural voice does not fit the duration instead of speeding up the voice or shifting its pitch.

## Check text, motion, and framing

- Watch the full video and list every on-screen text string with its start and end time.

- Remove any text the user did not approve and fix any text that appears in the wrong place or for the wrong duration.

- Confirm true motion when true motion was promised and name stills with slow zoom as stills when that is what the video contains.

- Check the thumbnail or first frame at delivery size for a black frame or a dark background or a cropped subject and fix the framing before delivery.

## Propagate a factual correction through every surface

- Treat every surface as uncorrected when a factual correction arrives after the script or voiceover is built, until you have checked that surface.

- Search the voiceover script and the title and the description and the chapter titles and any on-screen text for the superseded wording and for the claim it carried.

- Replace the superseded wording on every surface where you find it, and use only the corrected wording with its corrected scope and date.

- Re-record only the affected voiceover segment when the spoken claim is wrong, and re-render only the affected segment when that keeps the rest of the verified build intact.

- Hold the package back from public release while any surface still carries the superseded claim.

- Name the surfaces you checked and the segment you re-recorded when you report the fix, and name any surface you could not check.

## Report only what you verified

- State separately what you checked for faces and audio and voice and text and motion and framing.

- Name any check you could not run and do not call the video clean until that check passes.

- Send a compressed chat copy plus the full file only after every applicable check passes.

- Record the exact file you verified so a later resend or re-upload compares the same file.

- Ask the user to approve one corrected face or one corrected voice sample before you rebuild a whole batch.

- Offer the locked references and the verified prompt pack for user-side generation when direct generation is blocked.
