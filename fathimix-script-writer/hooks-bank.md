# FATHIMIX Hooks Bank — 21 proven hook formulas

Pick ONE per video, adapt the wording, never copy a competitor's line.
Every hook must pay off inside the video. Hindi examples are templates, not scripts.

1. **Numbered promise** — "GTA 6 mein 29 cheezein CONFIRMED hain — aur number 7 tumhein hairaan kar degi."
2. **Mistake warning** — "Ye galti ki, to GTA 6 ke pehle din hi phas jaoge."
3. **Hidden secret** — "Rockstar ne trailer mein ye chhupa diya tha — kisi ne notice hi nahi kiya."
4. **Versus** — "$80 wala edition ya $100 wala? Asal farq sirf itna hai."
5. **Countdown shock** — "Sirf kuch din bache hain, aur tum abhi tak ye nahi jante."
6. **Myth bust** — "Sab ye jhooth bol rahe hain. GTA 6 ka asal sach ye hai."
7. **Direct challenge** — "Agar tum GTA 6 khelne wale ho, to scroll rok ke pehle ye sun lo."
8. **Money at risk** — "Tumhare 80 dollar paani mein ja sakte hain — agar ye galti kar di."
9. **Nobody tells you** — "Jo baat GTA 6 ke baare mein koi nahi bata raha, woh ye hai."
10. **Before-you-play checklist** — "GTA 6 shuru karne se pehle ye 10 cheezein tayyar rakho."
11. **Map reveal** — "Leonida ka woh hissa jo Rockstar ne abhi tak chhupaya hai."
12. **Then vs now** — "GTA 5 mein ye hota tha. GTA 6 mein sab kuch badal gaya hai."
13. **Launch urgency** — "19 November se pehle ye na pata ho, to pehla hafta barbaad samjho."
14. **Big question** — "Kya GTA 6 PC par ayegi? Jawab seedha hai, lekin sun ke hairan hoge." (Answer honestly: no PC date announced.)
15. **Confirmed vs rumour split** — "Ye sab CONFIRMED hai — aur ye wali baat sirf RUMOUR hai, farq samjho."
16. **Character story** — "Lucia ki kahani woh nahi hai jo tum samajh rahe ho."
17. **Biggest change** — "GTA 6 ki sab se bari tabdeeli — jis ke liye koi tayyar nahi."
18. **Value trap** — "Ultimate Edition ka woh faida jo asal mein faida hai hi nahi."
19. **First hour guide** — "GTA 6 ke pehle ghante mein ye 5 kaam sab se pehle karna."
20. **Evolution** — "2001 se 2026 tak — GTA itna badal gaya ke pehchanoge nahi."
21. **Prediction with stakes** — "Launch ke pehle hafte mein ye hoga — likh lo, yaad karna mujhe."
