## The 10 signatures this proves

1. First frame is already a scene in motion — no intro, no logo, no title card.
2. A locked character pair (girl + sidekick creature) carries the story; the facts ride on their mini-adventure.
3. The camera ladder never repeats: wide → close → macro → cutaway → schematic → wide.
4. Every hidden part named in the narration is shown the second it is named (hinge, switch, relay, magnet, spring).
5. Neon highlight graphics (green arrows, outlines, diamonds, glowing wire pulses) point at the exact mechanism.
6. Branding lives inside the world (channel name printed on the chip), never as an overlay.
7. Captions are the narration itself: bold white + black outline, bottom-center, 2–4 word chunks flowing across shots.
8. Fake in-world props are fully designed (readable book covers) — the world feels real in close-up.
9. Palette contrast is saved for the payoff: bright room vs dark neon hidden room.
10. It ends on the reveal and stops. No outro, no ask — the loop and the comments do the work.
