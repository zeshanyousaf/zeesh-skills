# FATHIMIX 30-Day Content Calendar

Build and maintain a rolling 30-day content calendar for **FATHIMIX** (Hindi
GTA 6 faceless explainers; agent builds, Zeesh uploads Unlisted). Use when
Zeesh asks what's next, wants a month planned, or a video ships and the
calendar needs its next slot filled.

## Cadence (ColeTheMan formula)
- Fewer, longer deep-dives win: **3–4 videos per week maximum**, 8–18 min each.
  Never daily thin videos.
- Every slot names its ColeTheMan reference video to study first
  (hook-study rule — see fathimix-script-writer).

## Phases around launch (GTA 6 launches Thu 19 Nov 2026)
1. **Pre-launch:** confirmed features, editions/money guide, map, mistakes to
   avoid, before-you-play mega-guide.
2. **Launch week:** first-hour guide, day-one mistakes, story opening explained,
   settings/performance, what to do first in Leonida.
3. **Post-launch waves:** money methods, secrets/easter eggs, character
   breakdowns, map locations, comparisons with GTA 5, update/patch explainers,
   economy watch.

## Topic bank (rotate, don't repeat)
Confirmed features · Editions & price · Map/Locations · Mistakes · First-hour
guides · Story & characters (Lucia/Jason) · Money/economy · Secrets · GTA 5
comparisons · News/updates · Myths vs facts.

## Calendar format
Save to `~/workspace/your_files/fathimix/calendar-<YYYY-MM>.md` as a table:
| Date | Topic | Reference video to decode | Hook type (hooks bank #) | Title draft | Status |
Status values: planned → scripted → built → delivered → uploaded (Zeesh's step).
When a video is delivered, mark it, fill the next empty slot from the topic
bank, and tell Zeesh the next 3 upcoming topics in one short message.
