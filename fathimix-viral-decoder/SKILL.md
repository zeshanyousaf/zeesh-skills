# FATHIMIX Viral Decoder

Decode a viral or competitor GTA 6 video into a reusable brief for Zeesh's Hindi
faceless channel **FATHIMIX** (agent builds, Zeesh uploads Unlisted). Use this
whenever Zeesh shares a GTA video link, names a competitor video, or asks why a
video worked / to study a video before making ours.

Primary model channel: **ColeTheMan GTA (@colethemangta6)** — fewer, longer
15–21 min deep-dives, curiosity-gap titles, one big thumbnail concept.
Secondary reference: GameSight (thumbnail text style).

## Inputs
- A YouTube link, a video title, or just a topic ("map wali video decode karo").
- If only a topic is given, find ColeTheMan's video on that topic first.

## Workflow
1. **Gather public data** — title, channel, views, video age, length, like/comment
   signals. Use web/social search. Verbatim transcripts of competitor videos are
   NOT publicly available — work from public summaries, descriptions, chapters,
   comments and coverage. Never invent a transcript.
2. **Hook decode** — what promise is made in the first 5–10 seconds? What question
   is opened and left unanswered? Note the exact hook TYPE (numbered promise,
   myth-bust, versus, secret, etc. — see the hooks bank in fathimix-script-writer).
3. **Structure map** — segment-by-segment flow: opening promise → sections →
   where the strongest reveal sits → pattern interrupts → ending payoff/CTA.
4. **Coverage list** — every fact/claim the video covers, each labelled
   CONFIRMED (officially verified) or RUMOUR (leak/speculation). A leak-based
   claim is NEVER marked CONFIRMED in our version.
5. **Title decode** — curiosity gap mechanics, the one CAPS keyword, length.
6. **Thumbnail decode** — the single concept, word count, the one huge word,
   contrast, what makes it readable at small size.
7. **FATHIMIX application brief** — output a short brief with:
   - Borrow: the hook type, structure flow, coverage order (the FORMULA).
   - Never copy: their lines, wording, or jokes. Our script is original Hindi.
   - Our angle: what we add, correct, or do better for a Hindi audience.
   - Fact sheet: the CONFIRMED/RUMOUR list, ready for the script writer skill.

## Rules
- Facts about GTA 6 itself: launches 19 Nov 2026, PS5 + Xbox Series X|S only
  (no PC date announced), $79.99 Standard / $99.99 Ultimate.
- If public data can't establish something (e.g. exact hook wording), say so —
  decode what is verifiable, label the rest as inference.
- Deliverable is the brief, not a copied script. Hand off to
  **fathimix-script-writer** for the actual script.
