# FATHIMIX Title & Thumbnail

Produce the title pair and thumbnail set for a FATHIMIX GTA 6 video. Use with
every new video pack, or whenever Zeesh asks for title/thumbnail options.

## Title rules (owner-set 2026-11-14, ColeTheMan style)
- **Option A — Cole English:** full English, curiosity-gap style, ONE keyword
  in CAPS (the emotional/payoff word). No clickbait the video doesn't pay off.
- **Option B — Hybrid:** English phrase first + Devanagari hook, for Zeesh to
  choose at upload. Both options always delivered together.
- Title must match the video's actual strongest content — never promise a
  CONFIRMED fact that is only a rumour.

## Thumbnail rules (owner-set)
- **Text in ENGLISH** (GameSight style), even though the video is Hindi.
- **2–4 words total; ONE huge word at ~70% of the thumbnail width — never
  larger** (owner correction 2026-11-14).
- **GTA VI logo in a corner.**
- Palette: neon Vice City — hot pink / cyan / purple on dark.
- ONE concept per thumbnail. High contrast, readable at phone size.
- Thumbnail text is a hook of its own — it is NOT repeated inside the video.

## Deliverable
- Title Option A + Option B.
- **3 thumbnail variants (A/B/C)** with the primary pick marked and one line
  on why it wins (concept clarity + word size + contrast).
- Check each variant visually before delivering: word ≈70% width, logo in
  corner, no clutter, no second concept sneaking in.
